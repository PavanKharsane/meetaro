Safety, privacy & policies

Host review

Every host profile stays private until an administrator completes and records the required manual review. An approval label reflects the platform review process; it is not a guarantee of safety, identity, accommodation quality, or suitability.

Privacy by design

Search results never include a host’s legal name, email, phone, or exact home address. A host sees only a traveller’s display name and information relevant to a booking request. For an accommodation booking, the exact arrival address is shared with the traveller only after confirmation.

Bookings and cancellations

A request is not a confirmed booking. The host must accept first, and the traveller must confirm the final amount and availability. Contact the host through the platform to request a change or cancellation. Payment processing, taxes, refunds, and platform fees are not enabled in this prototype.

Food and accessibility

Share dietary requirements before requesting a meal; hosts must confirm whether a request can be met. Always discuss severe allergies and cross-contact directly before relying on a meal listing. For an emergency, contact local emergency services; Meetaro is not an emergency-response service.

Contact Meetaro

For questions or concerns, email meetaro@zohomail.in. You may include a booking reference so we can understand the issue. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, or identity documents.

Data and prototype limitations

Meetaro currently stores application data in a local JSON file for development. Use private, controlled storage for production identity documents; this app does not accept verification uploads, store payment card details, process payments, or provide an emergency response service.
